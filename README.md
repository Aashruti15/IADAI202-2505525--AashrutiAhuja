# Project Overview

FOOTLENS ANALYTICS is an interactive dashboard designed to study the relationship between football player injuries and team performance. It uses data analysis and visualisations to understand how player injuries, recovery periods and changes in individual performance are associated with a team's results.

The dashboard helps users explore injury patterns, compare player performance before and after recovery, and identify teams that may be more affected by player injuries.

# Problem Statement

Player injuries are a common challenge in football. When an important player is injured, they may be unavailable for upcoming matches, which can affect team selection and overall performance.

However, understanding the impact of injuries requires more than simply counting the number of injured players. Teams also need to examine how long players remain unavailable, how their performance changes after recovery, and whether team results differ during periods of player absence.

The aim of this project is to develop an interactive dashboard that analyses football injury data and explores the relationship between player injuries, recovery and team performance. It uses data visualisation to make these patterns easier to understand and interpret.

# Project Objectives

- Analyse injury patterns across different football players and clubs.
- Identify players and teams with frequent injuries.
- Compare player performance before and after an injury.
- Explore team results during periods when players are unavailable.
- Identify trends in player recovery and performance improvement.
- Present the findings through an interactive dashboard.

# Key Features

Overview Dashboard
- Displays important summary statistics.
- Shows total players, injuries and teams represented in the dataset.
- Provides an overall view of the injury data

Injury Analysis
- Identifies players with the highest number of injuries.
- Compares injury frequency across clubs.
- Examines injury patterns across different months.
- Highlights injuries associated with larger performance changes.

Team Performance Analysis
- Explores team results during player absence.
- Compares performance indicators across teams.
- Helps identify teams that may be more affected by player injuries.

Player Recovery Analysis
- Compares player ratings before and after injury.
- Identifies players who show improvement after recovery.
- Examines changes in performance over time.

Interactive Filters
Users can filter the dashboard using available dataset fields, such as:
- Club or team
- Player name
- Injury type
- Date or season
- Player age

Data Visualisations
The dashboard includes:
- Bar chart showing the top injuries associated with team performance drops.
- Line chart showing player performance before and after injury.
- Heatmap showing injury frequency across months and clubs.
- Scatter plot comparing player age and performance drop index.
- Leaderboard showing players ranked by rating improvement after recovery.

# Stakeholders


1. Football coaches - Can explore how player injuries are associated with team results and performance.

2. Team managers - Can understand injury patterns and consider their impact on team planning.
   
3.  Sports analysts - Can study player statistics, injury trends and performance changes.
  
4. Medical and physiotherapy staff - Can examine injury and recovery information to support discussions about player recovery.
 
5. Players - Can understand how their recorded performance changes before and after injuries. 

6. Club management - Can use the findings to understand potential performance challenges related to injuries. 

# Conclusion

FOOTLENS ANALYTICS aims to make football injury data easier to understand through data analysis and interactive visualisations. By comparing injury patterns, player performance and team results, the dashboard helps users explore how injuries may be associated with changes in football performance.

The project demonstrates how Python, data analysis and visualisation can be used to examine a real-world problem in sports. It also highlights the importance of using data to support informed discussions and decision-making in football.

The findings should be interpreted carefully because an association between injuries and team performance does not necessarily mean that injuries alone caused a change in results. Other factors, such as team strategy, opposition strength and player availability, may also influence performance.
